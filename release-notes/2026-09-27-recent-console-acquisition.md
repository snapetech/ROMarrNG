PS4, PS5, Vita, Xbox One and Xbox Series can now use the existing acquisition workflow for supported packages or folder dumps. Complete dumps retain their directory structure and download as one streamed archive. Platform aliases improve catalog matching, and large-file checksums use bounded memory. Acquisition does not establish emulator compatibility.
